1. Data We Collect

We collect account information you submit (such as name and email), operational metadata from workflows, connector request payloads, and billing request details required to process your selected plan.

2. How We Use Data

We use data to operate and improve workflow automation, provide support, secure the platform, route payment requests, and maintain activity timelines for operational traceability.

3. Data Retention

We retain data for as long as needed to provide services, satisfy legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. You may request deletion of eligible account data.

4. Security Controls

GlobalFlow uses encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, and audit logging. No internet system is fully immune to risk, but we apply reasonable safeguards for a platform of this scope.

5. Third-Party Services

Your workflows may interact with third-party platforms such as Slack, HubSpot, QuickBooks, Airtable, and payment providers. Their policies and terms govern how those services handle your data.

6. Your Rights

You can request access, correction, or deletion of personal data where applicable. You can also request export of account-level records maintained by the platform.

7. Contact

Privacy requests and questions can be sent to hello@globalflow.ai.
